## 📚 Key Legal Modules Covered
Get detailed legal analysis, punishment parameters, and bail status updates on the live portal:

### 1. New Criminal Laws (2024 Amendments)
Stay updated with the transition from colonial-era laws to the new Indian penal codes:
- **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS):** Complete replacement of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) (e.g., how Section 305 BNS/Section 318 BNS replaces old cheating and theft provisions like Section 380 IPC and Section 420 IPC).
- **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS):** Replaces the CrPC for modern criminal procedures.
- **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA):** Replaces the Indian Evidence Act.

### 2. Classic Penal & Civil Codes
- **Indian Penal Code (IPC) & CrPC:** Deep-dives into Section 380 IPC (Theft in dwelling house) and Section 420 IPC (Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property).
- **Civil & Corporate Laws:** Comprehensive guides on the Indian Contract Act, Specific Relief Act, Sale of Goods Act, and Indian Partnership Act.

### 3. Family Law & Domestic Violence
- **Domestic Violence Act (DV Act) 2005:** Comprehensive analysis of procedural rules, including Section 29 (Appeals), Section 30, Section 31 (Penalty for breach of protection order), and Section 32 of the DV Act.
- **Personal Laws:** Updates on the Hindu Marriage Act, Hindu Succession Act, and Family Courts Act.

### 4. Landmark Supreme Court Judgments
- Latest case summaries and rulings from the Supreme Court of India.
- Expert breakdowns of critical legal precedents (e.g., guidelines on anticipatory bail and conditions for recovery in cheating disputes).
